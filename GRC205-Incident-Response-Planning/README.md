## Scenario Overview

**Northbridge Skills Institute**, a regional vocational and professional education provider, identified anomalous activities indicative of a coordinated ransomware deployment targeting critical server infrastructure and student record databases. 

Acting as the **Incident Response Lead**, this assignment provides an end-to-end incident management strategy designed to triage, contain, eradicate, and recover from the compromise while ensuring business continuity, forensic readiness, and statutory regulatory compliance.

---

## Core Tasks Covered

The project deliverable addresses six critical domains of incident handling and resilience:

1. **Incident Classification & Severity Assessment**
   - Categorization of security events using structured severity matrices.
   - Assessment of operational disruption, data sensitivity, and threat scope.

2. **Incident Response Plan & Team Structure (CSIRT)**
   - Definition of the Cyber Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT) structure, core responsibilities, and operational hierarchy.
   - Cross-functional coordination involving IT, Security, Legal, Executive Leadership, and Communications.

3. **Response Playbook & Forensic Evidence Preservation**
   - Step-by-step technical procedures for ransomware triage, network segmentation, and endpoint isolation.
   - Adherence to forensic chain-of-custody protocols and volatile memory/log artifact preservation.

4. **Stakeholder Communication & Escalation Protocols**
   - Internal and external crisis communication workflows.
   - Defined escalation triggers, customer/student disclosure plans, and media management guidelines.

5. **Tabletop Exercise Design & Scenario Evaluation**
   - Scenario simulation testing institutional readiness and executive decision-making.
   - Evaluation metrics, exercise injects, and gap-identification methodologies.

6. **Case Study Analysis & Post-Incident Review**
   - Root cause analysis (RCA), timeline reconstruction, and post-incident reporting.
   - Continuous improvement strategy through corrective action plans and lessons learned.

## Regulatory & Technical Frameworks

The response architecture, playbooks, and governance strategies align with globally recognized standards and local regulatory requirements:

- **NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3**: *Computer Security Incident Handling Guide* - Framework used for structuring incident lifecycle stages (Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment, Eradication & Recovery, Post-Incident Activity).
- **CISA #StopRansomware Guide**: Technical guidance utilized for ransomware prevention, operational containment, and secure recovery.
- **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023**: Governance rules applied for data subject notification and mandatory 72-hour breach reporting to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
