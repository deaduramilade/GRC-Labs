# GRC205 Crisis Communication & Stakeholder Management Response Pack

This repository contains the final academic response pack for the GRC205 assignment on crisis communication and stakeholder management. It is designed to demonstrate how an organisation should communicate under uncertainty during a high-consequence cyber incident while maintaining trust, operational clarity, and regulatory discipline.

## Overview

The scenario centres on a suspected ransomware-related outage affecting a critical file share during payroll week. The objective is not to confirm a breach or speculate on attacker activity, but to show how a crisis team can communicate clearly, consistently, and responsibly while technical facts remain incomplete.

This response pack frames the issue as a strategic communication challenge, not merely a technical incident. It addresses how an organisation can:

- establish a single source of truth
- protect stakeholder trust under uncertainty
- control the narrative in the first four hours of an incident
- maintain operational continuity despite compromised communication channels
- escalate only when verified thresholds justify external disclosure

## Key Themes

### 1. Factual discipline
The pack strongly emphasizes that no claim should be made about ransomware status, data exposure, or payroll continuity unless it has been verified through the incident commander and legal review.

### 2. Stakeholder-specific communication
Messaging is tailored to the distinct needs of:

- employees
- executive leadership and the board
- payroll and banking partners
- key customers and B2B clients
- regulators and legal counsel

### 3. Out-of-band resilience
Because corporate systems may be compromised or unavailable, the plan prioritizes secure communication channels such as SMS, direct phone trees, encrypted bridges, and designated partner lines.

### 4. Controlled escalation
External disclosure is deliberately constrained to confirmed thresholds: verified data compromise, material service impact, or legal obligations. This reduces both legal risk and reputational exposure.
